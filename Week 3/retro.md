# Retrospective

A retrospective is a meeting at the end of a sprint where the team looks back at its own work. The team talks about what went well, what could be better and how to make it better in the next sprint.

There are many ways to hold a retrospective. In "Start, Stop, Continue, More of, Less of", the team lists things to start, stop, keep doing, do more of and do less of. In "Glad, Sad, Mad", everyone says what made them happy, sad or angry during the sprint, and the team looks for things to fix.

You can also use rounds, where one person writes everything down. First the team checks if the improvements from the last retrospective actually happened. Then everyone thinks quietly for a minute about one thing that went well, and shares it. The same is done for one thing that could be better, and one thing the team should stop doing. These rounds can be repeated if there is time.

At the end, the team agrees on at least three concrete improvements for the next sprint. They should be easy to check later, for example "every pull request is reviewed before merging".

A retrospective should take about half an hour and never more than an hour.
